# The Happy Man

– Aleko Konstantinov knows as "The Happy Man", is believed to have initiated the tourist movement in Bulgaria. A cosmopolitan traveler, he was on of the first Bulgarians to write about his visits to Western Europe and America.

This is an ongoing project of how I see the Vitosha mountain every time I visit my hometown. Born in Sofia, the mountain of Vitosha was a way of growing up. From a very young age (11y) I was a member of a mountain club for hiking, climbig and winter sports. 20+ years later I ended up living in Amsterdam, The Netherlands, one of the flattest places in the world, with no access to a mountain whatsover. Every year when I visit Sofia to see my friends and family, I would end up in the mountain.
